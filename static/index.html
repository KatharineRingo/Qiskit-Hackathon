How it works

  1. We score every road for hidden damage. King tides and a high water table soak the ground under a road. We combine tide exposure, groundwater depth and elevation into one risk score.
  2. We shortlist the roads that matter most. A damaged road on an evacuation or hospital route counts for more than one on a quiet side street.
  3. A quantum optimizer picks the best combination within your budget. Repairs on connected roads reinforce each other, so the best set is not simply the worst roads. This was computed ahead of time with IBM's Qiskit on a simulator.
  4. We compare it with today's approach: fixing the roads that get the most complaints.

Honest limits: this is a small problem, and a regular computer finds the same answer today. Real quantum hardware is still too noisy for city-sized problems. The point is that the method works and is ready to scale.
